hello world

curious about how broken systems can be put together better.

I love taking complicated, opaque, or gatekept problems and building straightforward, playful solutions around them. Whether that’s engineering stingray-resistant ocean footwear, designing robotic force-assisted carts for older adults, or automating media and retail intelligence to put money back in people's pockets.

My approach is pretty simple: listen closely to people who need help most, treat human rules as things that can always be rewritten, and use technology to level the playing field.

foundational pillars

things I stand for.

Core principles that guide what I build, where I spend my time, and what kind of future I want to help shape.

01 🤝

helping lower-income communities

Background, zip code, or family finances should never dictate a person's ceiling. Having grown up in a home where ends didn't always meet, I believe deeply in building systems, tools, and educational access that open doors rather than preserve barriers.

economic equity opportunity
02 ⚡

accessible technology

Tech is only truly revolutionary when anyone can use it without needing an engineering degree or a luxury subscription. I focus on removing friction, demystifying complex tools, and crafting interfaces that feel intuitive, empowering, and friendly.

universal design zero gatekeeping
03 🤖

working towards a positive AI future

Artificial intelligence shouldn't be about replacing human agency—it should amplify it. I’m building autonomous agent architectures that automate mundane drudgery so real people have more time for creativity, deep community, and genuine freedom.

agent workflows human agency
04 🌿

graceful aging & elder dignity

Society often overlooks older adults when designing modern products. From building force-sensing smart carts for senior mobility to advocating for intergenerational care, I care deeply about ensuring every person can age with physical safety, independence, and pride.

longevity tech dignity & mobility

Co-Founder // Ocean Innovation
UCSD & Beyond

DragonSkin Footwear

The world’s first stingray-resistant surfing bootie

Co-founded with college roommates at UC San Diego to solve a painful ocean safety hazard: stingray strikes. We turned our apartment into a testing lab, engineered a custom mechanical pendulum to replicate stingray barb impact forces, and formulated a flexible, proprietary puncture-resistant composite bootie. Shipped to hundreds of ocean lovers and featured across major press.
